# Mexico north pass 1

All 11 designs inspected in the rendered contact sheet at seed 42, phase 1.7. These are original geographic abstractions, not specimen reproductions.

- **aguascalientes**: Arcade readable; upper quiet area good; masonry needs detail.
- **baja-california**: Vine and hills read well; grid too similar to neighboring states.
- **baja-california-sur**: Rock arch too circular and diagram-like; needs irregular silhouette.
- **chihuahua**: Layered canyons legible; add geological vertical etching.
- **coahuila**: Pool is too perfect an ellipse; vary shoreline.
- **colima**: Volcano strong; palms look like fans, replace fronds.
- **durango**: Forest evenly repeated; vary heights/spacing.
- **nayarit**: Palms fan-shaped; replace with curved fronds.
- **nuevo-leon**: Saddle silhouette distinct; needs more ridge detail.
- **sinaloa**: Crop leaves repeat in rows; vary leaf angle and add furrows.
- **sonora**: Cacti clear; quiet space appropriate and warm palette coherent.
